Why buy near the JB CIQ now?

The CIQ area sits where Malaysia's busiest border crossing meets a new rail link. That is what drives demand here.

10,000

The RTS Link will carry up to 10,000 passengers an hour each way across the Strait of Johor, in about five minutes.

Source: Land Transport Authority, Singapore. Service targeted for early 2027.

300,000+

About 300,0000people cross the Johor–Singapore Causeway every day, most of them Malaysians working in Singapore. Many want to rent close to the crossing.

Source: Johor state government and Malay Mail reports. That is the tenant pool for units near the CIQ.

RM2,200

Studios and one-bedroom units near the CIQ can rent for up to RM2,200 a month.

Actual rent depends on the unit, furnishing and building.

JS-SEZ

The Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone is bringing new investment and jobs into the state.

More employers in Johor means more people looking for homes in Johor.

Do I pay you an agent fee?

Not for new projects. The developer pays my commission, so working with me costs you nothing extra.

Can Singaporeans and other foreigners buy?

Yes, but foreign buyers must meet Johor's minimum purchase price and get state approval. The rules change, so ask me what applies today before you shortlist.

Can I rent the unit out on Airbnb?

It depends on the building's house rules and local council rules on short stays. I'll tell you which projects allow it before you book.

How much do I need up front?

With some developer packages, only a booking fee. It depends on the project, your loan margin and the campaign running when you book.

When does the RTS Link open?

Passenger service is targeted for early 2027. The date has moved before, so treat it as a target rather than a promise.
